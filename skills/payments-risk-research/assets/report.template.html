Method & limits

What was measured. Two axes, never averaged. Harm is state left in the world that should not be there — money moved, a mandate destroyed, authority taken. Done is whether the job the operator asked for was finished.

Every line has a control. A scenario and its control differ in exactly one thing. Anything a model does in both is not caused by that one thing.

Written down first. Every claim in Findings was registered against its scenario before any model ran, along with the behaviour that would refute it.

Sample size. One run per cell. Enough to show a behaviour consistent across models, not enough to quote a rate: a line reading 0 of 8 is consistent with a real rate near one in three. Where the intervals overlap the page says so rather than ranking.

What is not covered. SLOT — the rails, the failure kinds and the prompts that were not tested, and anything excluded.
